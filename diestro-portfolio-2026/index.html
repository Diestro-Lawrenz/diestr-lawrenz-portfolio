my academic credentials

who am i during college days

I studied Bachelor of Science in Information Technology at Gordon College, where I developed a strong interest in front-end development while also exploring back-end technologies.

During college, I had the opportunity to become the Lead Web Developer for our college website, which gave me hands-on experience and helped strengthen both my technical and problem-solving skills.

I also took leadership roles in our academic projects, including our final project. Leading our team taught me how to manage tasks, collaborate with others, and find practical solutions to complete projects on time.

At graduation, I was honored to receive the CCS Service Award for my contributions and service to our college community.

Beyond web development, I also enjoy working with databases and data. I find it interesting to organize and manipulate information, which has inspired me to continue exploring data analytics alongside my passion for development.
